# Tower setup and check-in usability

Library protocols keep their published ID and version. Owners can record a
private “My version” note for their timing, dose, or other adaptation. Custom
protocols keep editable names and instructions.

Preset display names can be changed during setup or later. Preset keys, response
types, metric IDs, and existing readings are retained. Scales appear in setup and
alongside check-in inputs. Initial setup accepts one to 20 protocols and up to
12 total measures, including any combination of preset and custom measures.

Configured protocols appear on the Tower page. Existing library practice blocks
retain their reorder controls and dated practice records.

Sign-in preserves the requested protected page through email confirmation. For
cross-browser return, deploy the current Supabase magic-link and signup
confirmation templates. Both templates keep the explicit confirmation step.
